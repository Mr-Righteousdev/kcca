Citizen Feedback

Common Questions

How do I apply for a new trading licence? ▼
Apply online via the eCitie portal at ecitie2.kcca.go.ug. You will need your National Identification Number (NIN), registered business name, physical layout/address, and proof of premises occupancy. Registration fees depend on your specific business classification.
When is the trading licence renewal deadline? ▼
Licences must be renewed by 31 March each year. Late renewals attract a penalty surcharge. You can renew securely online at ecitie2.kcca.go.ug at any time to avoid cues and penalties.
How do I apply for a building or development permit? ▼
Submit your proposal files through the Smart Permit portal at kcca.iras.go.ug. You will need certified architectural drawings prepared by a registered architect. Letters or physical appeals should be formally addressed to the Director of Physical Planning, KCCA, P.O. Box 7010, Kampala.
Where can I find a registered architect? ▼
You can verify and contact licensed design professionals through the Architect's Registration Board of Uganda located at Plot 1B Kira Road (Kalamu House), or the Uganda Society of Architects at Plot 9 Agip House, Kampala Road.
How do I track my submitted plans? ▼
Track your submitted documents and reviews at the document tracker portal camcamv.kcca.go.ug by keying in your formal application reference code.
What fees can I pay online? ▼
Trading licence fees, city parking fee bookings, market stall dues, land property rates, and ground rent can all be settled securely on ecitie2.kcca.go.ug. Mobile Money (MTN/Airtel) and major credit/debit cards are accepted.
Where are KCCA's physical payment offices? ▼
Revenue counters are open at KCCA Headquarters, City Hall, Plot 1-3 Apollo Kaggwa Road, Kampala. Office operating hours are Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM.
How do I apply for a job at KCCA? ▼
All active public administration openings are published at kcca.go.ug/employment-opportunities. Applications are processed entirely online. KCCA does not accept walk-in resumes or physical folders.
Does KCCA offer internships? ▼
Yes, KCCA runs a structured attachment program. Eligible students can apply and review intakes at kcca.go.ug/internship.
How do I report an illegal construction? ▼
You can submit layout coordinates and violation photos on the KCCA ArcGIS survey portal, or contact the control division directly via the toll-free line: 0800 199 000.
How do I give feedback about a KCCA service? ▼
You can file official feedback directly using our website contact form below or visit kcca.go.ug/citizen-feedback. Emails can also be dispatched to info@kcca.go.ug.
